ቃል Amharic Keyboard

Privacy policy

Updated October 13, 2026 · App version 1.0.0

Regular keyboard input is processed on your device. Optional voice typing uses your device’s speech recognition provider. Handwriting recognition runs on your device after model downloads; Google ML Kit also sends technical diagnostics and usage metrics to Google.

About this policy

This policy describes how the Android app ቃል Amharic Keyboard (package com.geezigna.keyboard) accesses, uses, stores, and transmits information. It covers the app; websites and services you open from it have their own policies.

Typing and local data

The keyboard accesses text and editing context supplied by Android to provide typing, suggestions, corrections, and text editing. The app does not send regular keystrokes or the contents of text fields to a developer-operated server.

Preferences, custom dictionary entries, and keyboard configuration are stored locally. If clipboard history is enabled, copied text and supported media can be stored on your device for reuse. Text you enter is delivered to the app in which you are typing, which handles it under its own policy.

Optional voice typing

Voice typing requires microphone permission and starts when you activate the voice input feature. Audio is passed to Android’s configured speech recognition service to turn speech into text. Depending on that service, your device, and its settings, audio may be processed on remote servers. Offline recognition is not guaranteed.

The speech service controls its processing and retention under its own privacy policy. Recognized text is inserted into the current text field. The keyboard’s voice input implementation does not save microphone recordings. You can decline or revoke microphone permission and continue using regular typing.

Handwriting and Google ML Kit

Handwriting uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. Language models are downloaded from Google when needed. Handwriting strokes and recognition results are processed on your device and are not sent to Google for recognition.

ML Kit sends technical data to Google for diagnostics, maintenance, abuse prevention, and usage analytics. This can include device and operating system information, app package and version, installation identifiers, performance and error information, feature events and configuration, input/output sizes, and configured languages. Google documents HTTPS encryption for these SDK transmissions.

See ML Kit’s terms and privacy information, ML Kit’s data disclosure documentation, and Google’s privacy policy.

Permissions and network access

  • Microphone: optional voice typing, after permission is granted.
  • Internet: handwriting model downloads and SDK communications; speech recognition may also use online services.
  • Notifications: app notifications, if you grant permission.
  • Vibration: keypress feedback, when enabled.

The app can discover available keyboard and spell-checker services to support setup and switching. It does not require an account to type.

Backup, export, and sharing

Android backup or device transfer may copy supported preferences, keyboard files, and the local user dictionary, depending on your device settings and backup provider. You can also choose to export backups or share files and crash reports. The destination you choose receives the information you share. Review reports and backups before sharing them.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and dictionary entries remain until you change or delete them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Manage clipboard items in clipboard history and dictionary entries in dictionary settings. Clearing app storage removes app-owned local data and resets settings. Exported files, system dictionary entries, provider-held data, and backups must be managed separately through their respective apps or providers.

Google and your speech recognition provider determine retention of information they receive under their own policies. We do not control that retention.

Your choices and security

You can use normal typing without voice input, revoke microphone and notification permissions in Android settings, manage clipboard history, and control Android backup settings. App-owned data uses Android’s application storage protections. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Policy updates

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change and revise the date above. Review this policy when updating the app.
